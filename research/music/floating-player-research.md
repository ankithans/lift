# Floating player research — mobile correction

22 October 2026, logged-in Mobbin in Chrome. These pages were revisited during this fix. Static captures establish hierarchy and placement only: they do not establish background playback, integration support, gestures or animation. Existing phone references remain in `references/`; source links below are inspectable.

| App and inspected source | Observation | Lift decision |
|---|---|---|
| [Spotify](https://mobbin.com/screens/67fde0ef-addf-4773-a3e9-f31ada2dbfb2) | Song/artist and thumbnail remain distinct from large transport; output destination and queue are secondary. This particular capture is expanded now-playing, not evidence of a floating capsule. | Keep identity + Pause + Next in the small surface. Put device choice and queue in the sheet. Avoid Canvas, lyrics and a second header row. |
| [YouTube Music](https://mobbin.com/screens/d9b90824-ac50-4e7a-a306-28f3d2d0b39d) | Native Song/Video choice, title/artist, transport and Up Next form a stable order. | Borrow the audio-control hierarchy. The native Song option is not available to an embedded API client; retain a separate minimum-size video and preserve its attribution. |
| [Apple Music](https://mobbin.com/screens/cb958c7e-d09c-4a3c-95dd-4f14b2dbd066) | Compact identity precedes transport, while output and queue remain secondary even with lyrics open. | Two short identity lines, distinct sibling controls and a quiet raised surface. No lyrics or volume slider in the capsule. |
| [Peloton Strength+](https://mobbin.com/screens/873ed5bd-5d41-49f7-9c36-42e1bd04282c) | Session time and exercise dominate; adjustments stay near the exercise and Next is a separate lower surface. This is a workout reference, not proof of music integration. | Keep music outside the workout navbar and below the set task. RestStrip and keypad retain priority; music must reserve space rather than cover them. |
| [Strava collection](https://mobbin.com/apps/strava-ios-1ac190cf-46ee-4c65-845d-38bb8354219d/0581b139-5dc9-492d-89ad-da56951a027b/screens) | Inspected collection shows run metrics, maps, laps and a prominent activity Pause. No suitable floating music-player capture was established in this audit. | Do not borrow activity Pause semantics for music. The music capsule gets its own labelled controls and stays visually secondary to logging. No claim about Strava's current music behavior. |
| [Nike Run Club, Audio Player](https://mobbin.com/apps/nike-run-club-ios-449de884-1da3-4133-a57e-5106be737046/feeb6a15-da6e-4251-9d10-9abbc7eba89d/screens?filter=screenPatterns.Audio+Player) | The one filtered capture shows a narrow transport rail beside dominant activity metrics in a landscape run. | A compact control rail is enough. Reject the rotated layout and oversized music surface. |

The earlier [SoundCloud playlist capture](https://mobbin.com/screens/e15f41f6-ea7b-4c83-8466-5c3cd3c154b4) is the clearest existing persistent mini-strip reference in this research set. It supports keeping identity and immediate transport available while browsing; it does not justify placing controls over Lift's rest timer.

The implemented 48px capsule is Lift's synthesis of these references and the workout owner's exact spacing contract, not a pixel copy of a supposed Mobbin floating-player specification. It uses Inter, the existing raised and line tokens, 44px controls, no gradient/glow, and reduced-motion behavior. A separate 200×200 YouTube card is a policy constraint rather than a native app design imitation. See [policy and verification decisions](mobile-fix.md).
