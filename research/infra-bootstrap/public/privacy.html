Privacy policy

Effective October 22, 2026

Lift is a personal workout tracking app operated by Ankit Hans. This policy explains how Lift handles information when you visit the website or use the workout app.

Information you provide

When you sign in with Google, Lift receives your Google account identifier, email address, and basic profile information, such as your name and profile image. Lift requests only the openid, email, and profile permissions. It does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, or other Google account content.

When workout features are available, Lift stores the training information you choose to enter, such as exercises, sets, repetitions, weights, workout dates, and notes. Please avoid entering information that is unnecessary for your training log.

How information is used

Lift uses your information to sign you in, associate your workout history with your account, display your training records, maintain security, and respond to support or deletion requests. Google account information is used for authentication and account features. Lift does not sell personal information or use Google account information for advertising.

Service providers

Cloudflare hosts the website and processes requests, including technical information such as IP addresses and request metadata needed to deliver and secure the service. Convex provides the application backend, authentication, and storage for account and workout data. Google handles Google sign-in. These providers may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States.

Cookies and browser storage

Lift uses browser storage and authentication session information as needed to keep you signed in and operate the app. The current landing and policy pages do not set advertising cookies or include third-party analytics scripts.

Retention and your choices

Account and workout data are retained while your account is active and as needed to operate the service. You may request access, correction, export, or deletion by emailing ankithans1947@gmail.com from your account email. We may verify your identity before acting on a request. Limited records may be retained when needed for security, legal obligations, or provider backup retention.

You may stop using Lift at any time and revoke its Google access through your Google Account settings. Revoking Google access does not by itself delete an existing Lift account or workout records; contact us to request deletion.

Security and age

Lift uses encrypted HTTPS connections and account access controls. No online service can guarantee complete security. Lift is intended for adults and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

Changes and contact

This policy may change as Lift develops. The effective date above will be updated when the policy changes. For privacy questions or requests, contact Ankit Hans at ankithans1947@gmail.com.
