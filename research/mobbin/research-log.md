# Research scope and provenance

Research date: 22 October 2026. All online research used my own Chrome tab in the existing logged-in Mobbin session. Other agents' tabs and native apps were not operated.

## Passes
1. Inspected supplied Built With Science screenshots. Wrote first usable design-direction.md early, then continuously revised it.
2. Researched Peloton Strength+, Hevy and Fitbod logging. Downloaded Mobbin original screens through its UI; inspected contact sheets locally and selected meaningful states.
3. Compared Ladder and Bevel superset, rest, numeric entry, resume and finish flows.
4. Compared Oura iOS and Android; checked the web catalog and Linear mobile/desktop layouts.
5. Studied Raycast, Things, Cash App and Opal for restraint, sheets, keyboards and feedback; WHOOP and Nike Training Club for metric hierarchy and form/preview.
6. Focused final check on MacroFactor, Gentler Streak and Future Pro; final filenames, crop, provenance and spec QA.

## Catalog search results
- Directly reviewed collections: Peloton Strength+, Hevy, Fitbod, Ladder, Bevel, Oura (iOS and Android), Linear (iOS and Web), Raycast, Things 3, Cash App, Opal, WHOOP, Nike Training Club.
- Also directly reviewed in the final pass: MacroFactor, Gentler Streak, Future Pro.
- No exact app match surfaced in the iOS search suggestions used: Strong (returned Peloton Strength+), Caliber, Alpha Progression (returned other matches), StrongLifts. This is a bounded search result, not proof of catalog-wide absence.
- Web searches for Hevy and Strava returned an iOS collection but no web-app collection in the suggestions. Linear web was therefore used for desktop structure. Mobile captures must not be presented as responsive-web verification.
- Other apps in the user's example list were not individually audited. Scope favors a deep set-logging pass and a curated evidence set over a claim of exhaustive catalog coverage.

## Evidence limits
Screenshots prove layout, visible control labels and captured states. They do not independently prove a gesture, haptic, animation duration, sticky behavior, offline durability or background-timer reliability. Those behaviors in design-direction.md are Lift proposals. No mobile app was installed, no workout account was changed, and no real workout/health data was entered.

Capture dates range across years. Android Oura is December 2023 while iOS Oura is August 2026; platform differences and product redesigns are confounded. Older captures remain useful for interaction layout, not current feature availability.

## Files
The final screenshot set contains selected original-resolution PNGs with the Mobbin footer cropped off. Source collection URLs, screen URLs where recorded, original export filenames, dimensions and crop metadata are in manifest.json and screenshots.md. Uncropped originals remain in their original downloaded exports. The research folder contains the curated set only.
